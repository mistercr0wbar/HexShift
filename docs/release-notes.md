# Hex Shift 2

A focused update for recolouring emoji and transparent assets: a canvas-first responsive workspace, magnified picking, image palette, selection brushes, comparison views, redo, exact-colour and transparency modes, and lossless PNG/WebP export.

Images stay local. The update adds bounded input validation, guarded static SVG import, a restrictive content security policy, cancellable worker processing, scoped offline caches, and tighter Windows-host boundaries. Review docs/security-review.md in the source repository for verification evidence and remaining limitations.

**New in 2.5.0:** Colour map, recipes and batch. The Colour panel now holds a colour map: **+ Add colour** adds a row for each colour you want to change, each with its own selection, treatment and target, all applied together, so swapping red and blue no longer turns both the same colour. **Map all colours** fills the map with the image's colour families in one click, each kept as it is until you set a new colour. Where two changed colours meet, the blended edge pixels are remade from the two new colours, so a recoloured fill and outline meet cleanly, and gradients from one colour into another stay smooth. **Recipes and batch** (in Finish) saves your changes as a recipe file that repeats them on other images, applies a recipe to the open image, or applies your changes to up to 100 images at once into one ZIP at the size and format you chose. Recipes keep colours rather than places, so brush strokes and connected-region limits are left out, and they are read as data with strict size and range limits. Nothing new is stored.

**New in 2.4.1:** Cleaner recolours. Shift colour now gives every shade the target colour's hue, so a red-to-pink heart becomes one even blurple instead of drifting from violet to azure, and the blended pixels along a dark outline shift with the fill, so no rim of the old colour is left around it. Soft fades into other colours inside the artwork stay soft.

**New in 2.4.0:** Palettes, variants and skin tones. The target colour box now takes `rgb()`, `hsl()` and colour names as well as hex, and a new ◎ button picks a colour from anywhere on your screen (Chrome, Edge and the Windows app), such as the real colour of a Discord button. **Palettes and variants** (in Colour) replaces the Discord button list: choose Discord buttons, a 12-colour rainbow at equal brightness, the skin tones of Fluent, Twemoji, Noto or OpenMoji, or a palette of your own: open or paste GIMP, Lospec, JASC or Paint.NET palettes, or any list of hex colours, and save any palette as a `.gpl` file. Skin tones map the light and shadow shades of your selection onto each tone's colours, so a yellow emoji becomes a properly shaded skin tone in one click. **Make a variant for each colour** previews the current edit in every colour of the palette, and **Export all variants** saves them in one ZIP at the size you chose. Palettes are kept only while Hex Shift is open; nothing new is stored.

**New in 2.3.0:** Ship-ready exports. A new Size option in Finish makes files at the exact size each platform wants: Discord emoji and stickers, Slack emoji, a Twitch emote pack (112, 56 and 28 px in one ZIP), Telegram stickers and emoji, a WhatsApp sticker with its tray icon (one ZIP), YouTube emoji, or a custom size. The artwork is centred on a transparent square where the platform needs one, and a readout shows each file's size against the platform's limit before you export. A new small WebP format shrinks a file until it fits (WhatsApp stickers must be under 100 KB); it needs Chrome, Edge, Firefox or the Windows app. PNGs are about 40% smaller (measured on the built-in heart) with every pixel unchanged. SVGs open sharp at 512 px instead of their stated size (Fluent Color emoji were 32 px), with 256, 1024 and 2048 px under **SVG drawn at**, and SVGs with fractional sizes such as `width="32.5"` now open. The Windows app saves ZIP packs through the same Save As checks as images.

**New in 2.2.0:** Truer colour. With shading protection on, the colour you sample now lands exactly on your target (requested and achieved match) while the other shades scale around it. A new Perceptual colour model, on by default, keeps brightness when the hue changes; Classic HSL is one click away. Recolouring white or grey parts now gives one even tint instead of mixed tints. Make transparent gains Clean edges, which removes the colour from blended edge pixels so backgrounds leave no halo. A new Outline option (in Finish) keeps an emoji visible on a background of its own colour, such as a Discord button, and a real-size preview shows the result at 16–96 px on chat and Discord button colours. Large images process far faster: a 2048 × 2048 edit drops from many seconds to well under one.

**New in 2.1.0 (Windows):** Hex Shift can check for updates. At most once a day it asks the project's update address, and if a newer version exists it shows a notice with release notes, **Download update** and **Later**. Nothing is downloaded without a click, and nothing is installed without a second confirmation that reminds you to export your image first. Every update is verified with a cryptographic signature before it can run. Turn the automatic check off, or check by hand, from the window's system menu (right-click the title bar). There is now also a per-user installer, `HexShift-2.1.0-Setup.exe`; the portable program offers it instead of replacing itself. Copies of 2.0 have no updater, so install 2.1.0 once by hand. See docs/updates.md for how it works and how releases are signed.

**Also in 2.1.0:** SVGs with filter effects and blend modes now open, including Microsoft's Fluent Color emoji with their inner shadows. SVG filters that would draw in other images are still refused.

**Windows:** HexShift.exe is portable and currently unsigned. Verify its SHA-256 against SHA256SUMS.txt and its GitHub build provenance before use. A checksum hosted alongside a binary does not independently protect against a compromised publisher account. Do not routinely bypass operating-system warnings. No code-signing identity has been provisioned.

HexShift.html opens locally in a current browser. HexShift.zip includes the executable, HTML, documentation and licence notices.
